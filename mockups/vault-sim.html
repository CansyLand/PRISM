fundingpass-through · $0 to vault
Funding flows longs ↔ shorts (trader-to-trader) on-chain — it nets to zero for the vault. Its real job: it taxes the crowded side and pushes them toward the liquidation line, which is where the vault earns. Plus: fees, and — the structural edge — most leveraged traders lose.
