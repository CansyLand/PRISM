Is there leverage?

Nobody borrows and nobody can be liquidated. Curve carries built-in leverage of p: at p = 2 a 1% move changes its value by about 2%, but it can only shrink toward zero, never below it. Flat is a fully collateralized short of size p × Curve claims. There are no 10× shorts; the most anyone can lose is what they deposited.

Three directions, two volatility bets

Curve is bullish and long volatility. Flat is bearish and short volatility. Add a hedge and the direction disappears: Curve + short NEAR wins on big moves either way, and Flat + long NEAR is the sideways "Earn" position that collects funding when the market is quiet.

How the numbers are made

Curve units are worth NF × (P / P₀)p. NF decays at p(p−1)/2 × σ² per year; that decay is the funding. Flat owns the pool minus Curve claims. Hedges are rebalanced once a day at p × the current Curve value. Hedge trading costs and fees on the external market are left out.
