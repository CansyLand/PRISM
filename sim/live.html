Curve Live

Retail players buy Curve Longs with gold coins. Yield hunters deposit blue bars into the hedged vault. Funding drips from every open long into the vault, the vault trades BTC to stay hedged, and the exchange takes its cut. Change any number below while it runs.

Retail (Curve Long)
Each player bets 25% to 100% of their bankroll. A player who goes broke leaves and a new one arrives.
Yield hunters (hedged vault)
Each hunter wants roughly this yield (±40%). Above it they stay or come back; below it they leave and take their bars.
