Who gets liquidated?

Nine ways to draw the trader who loses everything to one wick, from a person to a mascot to a candlestick. Each card shows the same beat: the moment the position is gone.

Or no character at all. The chart is the hero: the wick, the stamp and the numbers carry the story (like B1). Faster to make, and it keeps the focus on the product. A character adds emotion and humor, and carries the hero journey from loss to win.
